# Art pipeline: swapping in real models

Every station, piece of furniture, food item and character is still created by code, so gameplay points
(WorkPoint, Seat_0..N, pantry shelf hitboxes, colliders) always exist. To replace the code-built LOOK with a
real model, put a prefab at:

    Assets/Resources/ArtOverrides/<Category>/<Id>.prefab

The code-built renderers are hidden and your prefab is placed at the same origin. Its colliders are disabled
(gameplay colliders stay on the original). Characters with an override lose the code-driven limb animation
unless the prefab brings its own Animator.

Orientation: +Z is the "front" (where a worker stands to use a station). Floor at Y = 0. 1 unit = 1 m; one
catalog tile = 1 m.
