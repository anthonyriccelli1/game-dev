# Physical kitchen playtest

This guide describes current source behavior, not a completed playtest of this checkout. Use an isolated save for testing; preserve the user's real save. See verification scope below.

Keyboard/mouse controls: WASD move, mouse look, Left Shift sprint, Space jump, E take/place/serve. First-person chopping and washing use the minigames below; elevated view retains hold-E work. Aim at individual pantry/fridge shelves to choose ingredients: Q no longer cycles the pantry or discards held food. Put unwanted food in the trash can with E. V changes camera, Tab opens management (truck reviews before leasing), B opens catalog/aimed furniture editing while service is closed, and R rotates in placement. P opens the phone, M the map. Left mouse attacks (hold/release for heavy hit) or flips a patty at the grill; right mouse blocks. Escape pauses.

For two-controller local play, connect both pads. Press Start on the first pad to assign Player 1, then Start on the second pad to join Player 2. Press A on Player 1's pad to start the game (service is opened separately). Left stick moves; right stick looks; A takes/places/serves and can be held to work; X also works at a loaded station. B cancels menus/minigames, not held-food disposal or pantry selection; use the trash can and aim at shelves. Y changes camera; left shoulder opens management; D-pad up edits aimed furniture; RT taps punch (hold and release for a heavy hit), LT blocks, and RB flips a patty while aiming at the grill. Start pauses outside shared menus. Player 1 controls shared menus with the D-pad or left stick, A to select, and B to close. The pause screen offers persistent 220/300/400 degree-per-second look-speed choices; 300 is the default. View/Select opens the phone after the stand is built; the phone has the map. In overhead placement, move the cursor with the D-pad or left stick, press RB to rotate, A to place, and B to return to the catalog. Keyboard and mouse remain available for Player 1.

For first-person kitchen minigames, aim at raw lettuce on the cutting board and press E/A. Move the mouse or either stick sideways to line up the knife with each uncut slice, then click or tap RT/RB for six successful cuts. Q/B puts the board down with partial progress retained. Aim at a dirty plate in the sink and press E/A, then move the mouse or either stick across the visible grime until clean; Q/B puts it down with partial progress retained. At the grill, wait for the underside to turn golden, aim at the grill, and click, flick the mouse upward, or tap RB to flip; press E/A to pick up the cooked patty when ready. Elevated view bypasses the chopping/scrubbing minigames and retains hold-to-work.

No worker is required to cook. Little Flame supplies a fixed starter kitchen. The Odd Table costs $350 and starts as an empty, shabby room: no free restaurant starter kit is installed on a fresh lease. Keep running the truck to buy pantry, grill, plate rack, assembly station, sink and seating; add a prep bench for salad and other equipment for expanded recipes. Aim at each station for its prompt.

- Flats Burger: raw patty goes directly from the pantry/fridge to the grill, with no prep step. A level-1 grill cooks it in 8 seconds; flip when the underside is golden and remove promptly. Total burn time is 24 seconds, but leaving one side down too long burns it earlier. Assemble cooked patty and bun on a clean plate, then serve the matching guest. A held plate can collect the cooked patty directly from its station and a bun directly from its shelf.
- Stoop Salad: chop greens on the prep bench and add them to a clean plate.
- Midnight Burger: find the purple strongbox on the tallest North Avenue roof after dark, once you own a restaurant. Call Zeeb on the phone for sauce, collect his drop in a grocery bag and unpack it. Add sauce directly from its shelf to a held burger plate; no sauce-prep step is needed for this shortcut.
- Planet Soup: buy the Cookbook recipe for $60 at 1 star and install a stove. Place soup vegetables in the pot, stir with E/A before the 9-second scorch threshold, and let it simmer for 14 seconds at level 1. Bring a clean plate to the stove to ladle the finished soup.
- Comet Dog: buy for $70 at 1 star; grill a sausage and plate it with a bun. Sausages cook and burn faster than patties and do not need flipping.
- Moonberry Float: buy for $110 at 2 stars; install the drink machine and buy moonberries. E/A sets a cup under the nozzle, hold interact to pour, then take and serve it before melting reduces quality.
- Cyclops Stack / Philosopher's Stack: recipes come from raids on Greasy Gus / The Alchemist. Plate bun, cooked patty and fried egg; Philosopher's Stack also needs chopped greens. Eggs fry quickly and need no flip. Follow the current station's occupancy prompt rather than assuming two ingredients fit on one grill.
- Clear a dirty plate from a table, place it in the sink, and scrub in first person (hold interact in elevated view). Stand plates return to the stand stack; restaurant plates return to its shared rack pool. The stand has four reusable plates. Restaurant capacity is at least four, with each rack contributing four/six/eight at levels 1/2/3; buying or upgrading racks increases capacity.

Open service from Tab management. The arrival window adapts to the restaurant's heat and stars: 130 seconds at difficulty level 0, increasing by 10 seconds per level up to 240 seconds. New arrivals are also limited to the shift's planned guest count. When the window ends, service closes to new arrivals while seated guests can finish. Interact with the door desk to close arrivals earlier. After the last guest leaves, the report shows sales, consumed ingredient value, wages, net, satisfaction, star change and staff energy. Lost guests include those who couldn't find a clean table. Losing a shift preserves your restaurant.

Recruited residents perform the assigned Cook, Serve or Clean job (Clean clears/washes); Stand assigns truck work, and Off rests. They use the same finite ingredients, plates and station slots as players. Resting restores energy; low energy slows work. If a worker waits at an occupied station, clear the blockage or help with the current dish. Source has worker recipe plans, but all current recipes have not been verified with staff in this checkout.

Talk to Milo (or Rose at Milo's cart near the truck) to open the Supplies cart, purchase ingredient packs, carry the bag home and unpack it. Basic packs contain six patties for $10, six buns for $4, or six greens for $6; the cart carries only those basics. The shop has recipe-gated ingredients. Shopping opens a shared panel: Player 1 owns its input and both players' gameplay input is suspended while it is open; active restaurant service continues. When broke and unable to cook or afford basics, Milo can top up patties, buns and greens to three each, once per day. Midnight recipe discovery does not itself award 3 Flux: night Flux cases award 1 each. Management offers permanent faster prep for 3 Flux or a tired worker energy boost for 1 Flux. Starting a restaurant shift at night enables +30% restaurant sales.

This is local co-op, not online multiplayer. The eight physical recipes above are implemented in source; Twin Moons, Dragon's Hoard and Glowshroom Melt are not ready. Soup is implemented and the stove is functional, not decorative. The oven's base patty cook time is six seconds; station upgrades change timing. Saves include cosmetic upgrades, workers, pantry and layout. Pre-version-3 saves receive a physical-migration backup (other backup conditions also exist); load sanitization closes transient service and recovers held plates. These are source behaviors, not save/reload results reproduced here.

## Checkout dependencies

`Assets/Synty/` (POLYGON City, Generic, Starter and Shops), `Assets/ThirdParty/` and `Assets/Resources/Mixamo/` are gitignored and absent in this checkout. Use entitled local copies for environment art and animation. The scene builder and resident animator have fallback paths, but the no-City builder branch omits the detailed city's raid targets and Flux/recipe spots. Full feature availability, visuals and animation without these files are unverified; do not treat the missing-assets checkout as equivalent to the art-complete build.

## Verification scope

The source acceptance harnesses use isolated state/saves and scripted game commands. `--controller-test` exercises startup and shared-menu input with two virtual gamepads without touching the real save. The physical harness also tops up funds to furnish the empty restaurant and grants some unlocks directly; it is not an end-to-end human progression test or coverage of every current dish.

`docs/PHYSICAL_VERIFICATION.md` records historical Windows scripted results (including `PHYSICAL_RUNTIME_PASS 252` and `PHYSICAL_RESUME_PASS 2`); `docs/VERIFICATION.md` records an earlier smoke build. Those records were not reproduced or their original logs inspected here. The Windows executable and `Acceptance/` logs are absent in this checkout. This documentation update used source inspection only: no Unity, build, runtime acceptance, physical gamepads or human play session. Controller button feel, simultaneous human coordination, all-recipe/staff behavior, missing-assets fallbacks and a complete 20–30 minute human session remain unverified on the user's hardware.
