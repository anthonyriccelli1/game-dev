# First playtest checklist

The prototype is successful when a new player can complete this sequence without developer help. Automated tests cover state invariants; this checklist covers the experience.

- Enter Play Mode, read the introduction, start a shift, move and look around.
- Pause with Escape; verify the mouse is released and the cooking/order clocks stop. Resume.
- Find the coral stand, spend $10 to set it up, and locate the supplier.
- Buy stock, prep one burger, grill it, plate during the 4–10 second window, and serve a waiting guest.
- Deliberately burn a burger and use the bin to recover.
- Let one customer's patience run out; continue serving the next guest.
- Watch dusk arrive and compare a daytime payment with a night payment.
- Enter the rival alley, observe a windup, back away to avoid a strike, and retreat to the street.
- Defeat the rival with three spatula hits, claim the recipe, and sell a midnight burger.
- Be defeated once and verify the stand and learned recipe remain after safe respawn.
- Quit and reopen, confirming cash/stock/unlocks load. Orders and partially cooked food intentionally reset.
- Save $150 and visit the future restaurant sign.

Record: confusing prompts, travel that feels too long, mouse sensitivity, unreadable signs, unclear hit feedback, and whether another shift feels appealing. No manual-playtest results have been claimed by the implementation agent.
