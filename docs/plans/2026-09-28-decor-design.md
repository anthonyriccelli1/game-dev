# Restaurant decor design

Approved direction: the user agreed to prioritize customization, individual wall sections/floor tiles, richer choices and progression unlocks. This first runnable stage replaces the five-finish bottleneck before expanding wall furniture.

Combine a substantial finish catalog with local application. Palette-only expansion would leave the entire-room click problem; a full purchased asset pipeline is premature. Use original procedural textures with clear material differences rather than relying on recolors.

Target twelve wall choices and ten floor choices total, including existing stable IDs. Walls include plaster/paint, stripes, patterned paper, brick and paneling. Floors include checker, wood, ceramic, terrazzo and more premium materials. Affordable basics are available immediately; statement finishes unlock through existing restaurant stars and city reputation ranks. Do not add another XP currency or copy Zombie Cafe art.

Select a finish without spending money. Enter a brush view with a clear surface preview; click a floor tile or wall section to purchase that patch. Repeated application of an identical finish is free/no-op. Whole-room fill is explicit and priced before applying. Esc cancels without charging. The brush remains active for repeated application. Finish purchases cannot create arbitrary ambience per tile: contribution is bounded by actual surface coverage. Existing whole-room finish saves remain the base underneath additive surface overrides.

New finish records store stable surface keys and catalog IDs. Invalid entries and duplicates are sanitized on load. Service must be closed for customization, and cash/locks are revalidated when applying. Layout rebuild and save/load restore exact patch choices. Finish overlays add no physics collider or navigation blockage.

Wall-mounted furnishings and further furniture collections are the next stage; this stage proves substantial material choice, mixing designs, understandable unlocks and persistent finishes in a built player.
